On-Device Crash Test

Crash-test your on-device AI before your customers do.

Problem: The compressed INT8 detector passed lab validation on bright, static photos and fails silently under couches at night, in window glare, or with steam on the lens.

What a repair is: A re-quantization recipe (calibration set, calibration method, per-channel granularity, node exclusions) verified by a zero-regression gate under a hard compression budget. No training, no labels.

Baseline recipe: Quantized the way teams do: calibrated on the 25 brightest, sharpest lab-like images of Split A (MinMax, per-tensor, stretch preprocessing, no exclusions). Every number reported below is measured on held-out Split B (40 images).
