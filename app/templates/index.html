COCO val2017 Scenario Pool (Split A 40 / Split B 40) Conv-Only INT8 QDQ Zero Labels / No Training

Problem: The compressed INT8 detector passed lab validation on bright, static photos and fails silently under couches at night, in window glare, or with fog/steam on the lens.

What a repair is: A re-quantization recipe (calibration set, calibration method, per-channel granularity, node exclusions) verified by a zero-regression gate under a hard compression budget. No training, no labels.

Calibration vs. evaluation boundary: Data pool is 80 COCO val2017 images containing the scenario classes (people included), split into 40 Split-A calibration images and 40 held-out Split-B evaluation images. Baseline INT8 is calibrated on the 25 brightest, sharpest lab-like images of Split A (MinMax, per-tensor, Conv-only QDQ, stretch preprocessing, no exclusions). Calibration only touches Split A and Split-A condition images; every reported metric is measured on held-out Split B (40 images for baseline control and full matrix; requested n_images for live runs).
